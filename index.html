    Learn to read sheet music · step by step

    Music is written on a staff of five lines. Every line and every space between them is a note, and the higher it sits, the higher it sounds. Going up, the letters run C D E F G A H, then start again. A sharp ♯ raises a note a half step to the one in between: F♯ is Fís in Icelandic.

    Note
    —
    Tap a note on the staff to hear it and see where it is on the violin.
    Remember
    • Lines — from the bottom: E, G, H, D, F. (In English H is B: “Every Good Boy Does Fine”.)
    • Spaces — from the bottom: F, A, C, E. They spell “face”.
    • Coloured notes — your open strings: G under two ledger lines, D just below the staff, A in the 2nd space, E in the 4th space.

    The shape of a note shows how long it lasts, counted in beats. Each row fills one bar of four beats. Tap a row to hear it against a steady beat, and tap again to stop.

    Every note has a rest of the same length, shown on the right: a silence where the bow stops.

    Besides the notes, the page is full of small signs that tell you how to play them. Test yourself under Quiz › Signs.

    An interval is the distance between two notes, counted in letters: A to C♯ is a 3rd (A, H, C). On the violin you feel it as how far your fingers move. Tap a card to hear the two notes one after the other, then together. Test your ear under Quiz › Intervals.

    A half step is the smallest step, fingers touching; a whole step leaves a finger’s width between them. Every bigger interval is made of these.

    On each string your fingers sit a whole step apart, a finger’s width between them, or a half step apart, touching. Where they touch makes the finger pattern: with high 2 the 2nd finger touches the 3rd, with low 2 it touches the 1st. Tap a string to hear it from the open string up to the 4th finger. Test yourself under Quiz › Find it.

    Which pattern a string takes depends on the key. A major (Twinkle to Long, Long Ago) has high 2 on the D, A and E strings: F♯, C♯, G♯. G major (the minuets, The Happy Farmer) has high 2 on the G and D strings and low 2 on the A and E strings: C and G. D major has low 2 only on the E string.
